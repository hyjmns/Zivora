Does ZIVORA offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across Pakistan — you pay at your doorstep when your bag arrives. Bank transfer is also accepted at checkout.

Delivery is PKR 250 per order, and completely free on orders over PKR 5,000, anywhere in Pakistan.

Supabase: connection issue — showing website products
Owner Login

Admin Sign In

Managing products and viewing customer orders is for the store owner only. Sign in with the email and password of your Supabase account — the account that owns the ZIVORA project. If you don't have a user yet, create one first in Supabase Dashboard → Authentication → Users.

Supabase permission fix (one-time only):
The tables exist, but the website's anon key does not have access yet — that is why the connection shows “permission denied”. Run this in Supabase Dashboard → SQL Editor, then press Refresh here: 
alter table public.products enable row level security;
alter table public.orders enable row level security;
grant select on public.products to anon;
grant insert on public.orders to anon;
grant select, insert, update, delete on public.products to authenticated;
grant select, update on public.orders to authenticated;
grant usage, select on all sequences in schema public to anon, authenticated;
drop policy if exists "zivora products public read" on public.products;
create policy "zivora products public read" on public.products for select to anon, authenticated using (true);
drop policy if exists "zivora products owner manage" on public.products;
create policy "zivora products owner manage" on public.products for all to authenticated using (true) with check (true);
drop policy if exists "zivora orders public insert" on public.orders;
create policy "zivora orders public insert" on public.orders for insert to anon, authenticated with check (true);
drop policy if exists "zivora orders owner read" on public.orders;
create policy "zivora orders owner read" on public.orders for select to authenticated using (true);
drop policy if exists "zivora orders owner update" on public.orders;
create policy "zivora orders owner update" on public.orders for update to authenticated using (true) with check (true);
alter table public.orders add column if not exists email text;
With this, the public can only view products and place new orders — reading orders and changing products stays limited to the signed-in owner.

We believe a handbag is more than just an accessory. It's a reflection of your personality, confidence, and individual style. That's why we bring you a thoughtfully curated collection of ladies' bags designed to complement every occasion, outfit, and lifestyle.

From elegant handbags and trendy shoulder bags to stylish side bags, chic clutches, and versatile backpacks, Zivora offers something for every woman who loves to express herself through fashion.
